⚙️ Settings

📚 My ESSAz

Save multiple ESSAs and switch between them whenever you like. Each ESSA keeps its own name, species, voice, and photo.

Save an ESSA to keep it for later. Changes to a saved ESSA can be saved again.

🖼️ Your ESSA Photo

Choose a photo to replace the center emoji. Your image stays on this device.

Photos are stored locally when your browser has space available.

🐾 ESSA Soundz

Choose a companion species, then add the matching recordings to the sounds folder. Press Speak or Eat, or enable voice commands to say “speak”, “eat”, “no”, or “wait”.

Say your ESSA’s name to get their attention. Name reactions have a 3-second cooldown.

Animal audio files are not bundled; see SOUND-CREDITS-AND-SETUP.txt.

💡 Tips

Use HTTPS or localhost and allow microphone access when prompted. Bluetooth speakers can play your music, but Echoz still needs your device's microphone to hear it. Some phones won't capture sound from a connected speaker if the microphone is muted or routed elsewhere.
