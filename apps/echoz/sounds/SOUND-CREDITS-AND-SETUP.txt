ESSAzLife Echoz v1.2 — FREESOUND RECORDING GUIDE

Download recordings yourself from each creator's Freesound page.
Check the license on the page before redistributing recordings.
Convert WAV/M4A files to MP3 as needed; changing the extension alone does NOT convert audio.
Save these exact filenames inside the sounds/ folder:

dog-bark.mp3  https://freesound.org/people/WolfOWI/sounds/588312/
dog-whine.mp3 https://freesound.org/people/MiscPractice/sounds/652711/
dog-sleep.mp3 https://freesound.org/people/samueljustice00/sounds/156597/
heartbeat.mp3 https://freesound.org/people/loudernoises/sounds/332821/
puppy.mp3 https://freesound.org/people/johnnypanic/sounds/728029/
dog-eating.mp3 https://freesound.org/people/Trashcan_Studios/sounds/580210/
kitten-meow.mp3 https://freesound.org/people/designerschoice/sounds/803590/
cat-meow.mp3 https://freesound.org/people/MaximStudio_official_sounds/sounds/856618/
cat-purr.mp3 https://freesound.org/people/ChExi/sounds/646599/
cat-eating.mp3 https://freesound.org/people/patchytherat/sounds/532216/
cow-moo.mp3 https://freesound.org/people/Cllari/sounds/445572/

Sleep uses dog-sleep.mp3 for dogs and cows, cat-purr.mp3 for cats.
Heartbeat uses heartbeat.mp3 for all species.
Cows have no eating or no/wait sound assigned.
Optional voice recognition may send speech to the browser's speech provider.
Audio files are NOT included in this ZIP.
