❓ Chorez Help

Everything you need to know about keeping yourself — and MooCow — on track.

🐄 How Chorez Works

Add chores to your Chorez list and tap Done when you complete one.

Every completed chore earns you 1 Chorez Coin.

Visit MooCow and use your coins to get food from his fridge. Feed him to keep his hunger meter filled.

🧳 What If MooCow Gets Too Hungry?

MooCow's hunger decreases over time. If it reaches 0%, he may pack his little suitcase and go stay with Voidy.

Complete chores to work toward getting MooCow back.

📅 Chore History

Open Chore History to view your calendar.

Select a date to see which chores were Done and which chores from that date are still Not Done.

📱 Your Account Is Local

Chorez stores your account and progress on this device instead of an online server.

Clearing your browser data or switching devices can cause you to lose your Chorez data unless you have a backup.

💾 How to Back Up Chorez

Open Profile and select Backup Chorez.

Keep the downloaded backup file somewhere safe. It contains the information Chorez needs to restore your local account and progress.

📂 How to Restore a Backup

Open Profile and select Restore Backup.

Choose a Chorez backup file you previously created and follow the restore instructions.
