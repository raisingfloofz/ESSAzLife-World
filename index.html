❓ ESSAzLife World Help

▶️ Watch Tutorial

🌎 What is ESSAzLife World?

ESSAzLife World is the headquarters for ESSAzLife apps. It gives you one place to access Handler Edition, PlayMode, Chorez, and future ESSAzLife apps.

📱 Do I need World?

Nope! ESSAzLife World is optional. You can use World as your all-in-one ESSAzLife app, or use the individual ESSAzLife apps separately.

🔗 What is Directory?

Directory shows the individual ESSAzLife apps, what they do, and where you can access each standalone app.

💾 Where is my data stored?

ESSAzLife apps store their data locally in your browser. Your data does not automatically follow you to another device or browser. If you choose to change devices or browsers, or clear browser history, you will need to get your "Backup" file from Profile in the individual app. Take your Downloaded Backup file and restore it in the specific app under profile. Anything from that backup file will be restored on the new device, however, these will not sync.

⬇️ What is a PWA? (A Message from the developer)

A Progressive Web App (PWA) is a website that can be installed on your device and used more like an app. ESSAzLife World and the individual ESSAzLife apps can be installed as PWAs when supported by your browser and device. PWA is used for all ESSAzLife Apps because if I were to upload the apps to Play Store or App Store, I would be unable to make these apps available for everyone to enjoy.
